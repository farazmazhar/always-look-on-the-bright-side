# Lecture 2 — AWS PartyRock: Build Your First AI Chatbot

**No coding required.** Free, no AWS account or credit card needed.

## What is PartyRock?

AWS PartyRock is a no-code platform for generative AI development — an all-in-one playground to build AI chatbots powered by LLMs with a drag-and-drop UI.

## What was built

A Master Yoda-style conversational AI assistant, in under 5 minutes, with zero lines of code.

## Key steps

1. Create an account at [partyrock.aws](https://partyrock.aws)
2. Configure the chatbot (personality, tone, speaking style)
3. Test with real-time user interactions
4. Deploy & share

## Why it matters

- Proves you can spin up a functional AI chatbot without coding
- Introduces LLMs and natural language processing concepts via a hands-on demo
- Sets up the "no-code" baseline before diving into Python/PyTorch later in the course
